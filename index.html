Type the word, click Record, then hold the sign in front of the camera. It records for 3 seconds after a 3-second countdown. Record a few times, slightly moving or tilting your hand, for better accuracy. Use both hands if the sign uses both.

⚙️ Matching strictness

Lower = stricter. Raise it if your own signs are not being recognised, lower it if it guesses wrong.

ℹ️ Built-in signs

  • 1 finger → One
  • 2 fingers → Two
  • 3 fingers → Three
  • 4 fingers → Four
  • Open hand → Five / Hello
  • Fist → Yes
  • Thumb up → Good
  • Thumb + little finger → Call me
  • Thumb + index + little finger → I love you
  • Both palms together → Namaste
  • Both open hands apart → Welcome
  • Both thumbs up → Very good
  • Both fists → Stop
